# Subscription Management System

### A Unified Platform for Subscription and Recurring Billing Management

**Team Name: VIANRA**

## About the Project

The Subscription Management System is designed to simplify and automate the complete subscription lifecycle of a business. It provides a centralized platform to manage customers, products, subscription plans, recurring invoices, payments, discounts, and revenue reports.

The project addresses common challenges in subscription-based businesses, such as manual invoice generation, missed renewals, payment tracking difficulties, and inefficient revenue reporting.

## Problem Statement

Businesses that operate on subscription-based models often rely on spreadsheets and manual processes to manage subscriptions, renewals, invoices, and payments. These processes can lead to errors, missed payments, duplicate invoices, and poor financial visibility.

Our goal is to develop a unified system that streamlines these operations and improves billing accuracy, transparency, and efficiency.

## Key Features

- **Customer Management:** Maintain customer information and accounts.
- **Product Management:** Create and manage products, pricing, and product variants.
- **Subscription Management:** Create, track, pause, renew, and close subscriptions.
- **Recurring Billing:** Generate invoices based on subscription billing cycles.
- **Invoice Management:** Track invoice status, taxes, totals, and due dates.
- **Discount Management:** Apply fixed and percentage-based discounts.
- **Payment Tracking:** Record payments and monitor outstanding balances.
- **Analytics Dashboard:** View active subscriptions, revenue, payments, and overdue invoices.
- **Role-Based Access:** Support different access levels for administrators, staff, and customers.

## Subscription Lifecycle

Draft → Quotation → Confirmed → Active → Closed

## Project Objectives

1. Centralize subscription and billing operations.
2. Reduce manual work through automated billing processes.
3. Improve payment tracking and invoice management.
4. Minimize billing errors and duplicate invoices.
5. Provide financial insights through analytics and reports.
6. Improve the experience for administrators, staff, and customers.
